THE DESIGN OPPORTUNITY

From alert overload to operational clarity.

Enterprise teams rarely lack data. They struggle to connect signals, understand impact and decide what to do next. The experience should make service health legible, surface what matters and support action without hiding the evidence.

01 · Prioritise

Bring high-impact incidents forward instead of presenting every alert with equal weight.

02 · Explain

Connect symptoms to affected services, recent changes and supporting signals.

03 · Guide

Make the next best investigation step visible while keeping the operator in control.

INTERACTION EXPLORATION
