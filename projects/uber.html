JOURNEY MOMENT

Set a clear pickup point

Users may be unsure which entrance or gate to select.

Research lens: Pickup clarity. Validate this hypothesis through Bengaluru rider interviews and observed trip walkthroughs.

RESEARCH FRAME

Look beyond the booking confirmation.

A ride-hailing journey spans intent, pickup coordination, driver matching, in-trip confidence and issue resolution. The opportunity is to understand the moments where expectations and reality diverge—not to assume that every inconvenience is an interface problem.

Pickup ambiguity

Can riders and drivers identify the same pickup point in complex locations?

Trust and confidence

Do vehicle, driver and trip details answer the rider's immediate questions?

Change communication

Are ETA, route and pickup changes explained at the moment they matter?

VALIDATION PLAN

Turn hypotheses into evidence.

Next research steps: recruit Bengaluru riders across commute and occasional-use segments, conduct contextual interviews, observe booking and pickup tasks, review app-store feedback, and rank issues by frequency, severity and confidence. Findings on this page are hypotheses until validated with participants and cited evidence.
